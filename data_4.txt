The dataset is a synthetic dataset representing individuals’ gaming behavior, lifestyle habits, psychological condition, social environment, and physical health indicators.

The dataset contains variables grouped into six main domains.

Demographic Features
age — Age of the participant
gender — Gender category
income — Monthly income estimate

Gaming Behavior Features
daily_gaming_hours — Average daily gaming time
weekly_sessions — Number of weekly gaming sessions
years_gaming — Total years of gaming experience
weekend_gaming_hours — Weekend gaming duration
multiplayer_ratio — Fraction of multiplayer gaming
violent_games_ratio — Fraction of violent games played
mobile_gaming_ratio — Fraction of mobile gaming
night_gaming_ratio — Proportion of night-time gaming
competitive_rank — Competitive skill ranking
esports_interest — Interest in esports participation
streaming_hours — Weekly game streaming hours
microtransactios_spending— Monthly in-game spending
headset_usage— Whether headset is used

Psychological Health Features
stress_level — Perceived stress score
anxiety_score — Anxiety assessment score
depression_score — Depression score
addiction_level — Gaming addiction indicator
loneliness_score — Loneliness index
aggression_score — Aggression tendency score
happiness_score — Overall happiness level

Social Environment Features
social_interaction_score — Social activity index
relationship_satisfaction — Relationship quality score
friends_gaming_count — Number of gaming friends
online_friends — Online social connections
toxic_exposure — Exposure to toxic communities
parental_supervision — Parental monitoring score

Lifestyle Features
sleep_hours — Average sleep duration
exercise_hours — Weekly exercise hours
caffeine_intake — Daily caffeine consumption
screen_time_total — Total daily screen time
internet_quality — Internet connectivity quality

Physical Health Indicators
bmi — Body mass index
eye_strain_score — Eye strain severity
back_pain_score — Back pain severity

Productivity / Performance Indicators
academic_performance — Academic score
work_productivity — Workplace productivity score
